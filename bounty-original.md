Follow and tag [@SteveTheAgentAI](https://x.com/SteveTheAgentAI) and [@OOBEonSol](https://x.com/OOBEonSol).

### 3. Reach 1,000 Arena XP

Reach at least **1,000 Arena XP** before the competition closes.

### 4. Complete At Least 5 Trades

Execute at least **5 qualifying trades** through your Steve Agent on Solana Mainnet:

- Adrena perps

Trades must be executed through your agent wallet. Wash trading and self-dealing do not count.
